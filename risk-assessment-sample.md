# Risk Assessment Sample

Example of how a cybersecurity team documents risk during a governance and compliance assessment.

---

## Risk ID: RISK-001

Risk Description:
Unauthorized access due to weak authentication policies.

Likelihood:
Medium

Impact:
High

Risk Level:
High

Mitigation Strategy:
Implement multi-factor authentication across all user accounts.

---

## Risk ID: RISK-002

Risk Description:
Security incidents not detected due to lack of centralized monitoring.

Likelihood:
Medium

Impact:
High

Risk Level:
High

Mitigation Strategy:
Deploy centralized logging and monitoring infrastructure.
